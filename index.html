From One Naturalist to Another

Hey there! I created this game as a labor of love for history, geology, and our majestic National Parks. I'm a stay-at-home dad dedicated to teaching my two wonderful kids about the wonders of the natural world. My dream is to pack up my family—my incredible wife and our two little adventurers—and visit all 63 US National Parks together in person.

Our absolute top dream destination is Zion National Park—to walk between those towering red sandstone canyon walls and stand beneath the desert stars together. If you're enjoying your time combing these virtual landscapes, cataloging relics, and learning about our heritage, any support toward our family's park expedition fund means the world to us!
